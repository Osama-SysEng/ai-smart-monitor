# Source Alignment

The uploaded `Al-La'eeb (1).zip` was used as an architectural/reference baseline only. Its organization patterns (FastAPI services, shared configuration, PostgreSQL/Redis infrastructure, monitoring, CI/CD conventions) informed this independent project.

The domain was intentionally replaced: this archive is **The Smart Monitor**, not Al-La'eeb.

The football/player domain, video scouting, player models and sports UI are not copied as business functionality because they do not belong to the Smart Monitor specification.
